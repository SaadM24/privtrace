# Method and threat model
The four sites are Alder (north/coast), Birch (north/inland), Cedar (south/coast) and Dune (south/inland). Each disclosure advertises an admissible set of sites. Gold knowledge at an observer is the intersection of all sets already delivered to that observer. Disclosure means a transition from at least two feasible sites to one. Staff has clearance for identification but still requires a live grant for sealed data. Prior grants and already delivered material are never retroactively rescored. A preview purpose is insufficient for a substantive copy even when the source is routine. The synthetic signed statements always refer to the true site in the scripted workflow; separately ambiguous utterances and irrelevant mentions are evaluated in the language assay.

Exact task success depends on distinct signers early, individual required signers late, and optional commissioning of a staff document. Signers may share mailboxes. Shared delivery implies shared recipient knowledge but does not turn two signatures into one. A verifier outage affects selected jobs' later certificate/document offers. Bad positive evidence can mask a sealed root and omit a certifier's history. The checked method's observed zero exposures with correct proof is conditional on these declared semantics and complete mediation. Trusted history for the language-gate ablation isolates current-message interpretation. The release models delivery gateways, not transmission timing, uninstrumented collusion or key theft.

The held-out semantic assay uses 51 determinate strings and 195 consistent prior/message pairs. Accuracy on answered rows is conditional; include abstention on positive and negative examples. Two ambiguous strings recur from development; neither is used for fitting or included in determinate scores. The dictionary can recognize some held-out wording by design. Logistic regression learns only from development. No test labels were used to choose parameters. The benchmark gold is authored at template level and not externally annotated. Source-level scorer independence does not remove construct bias.

Numerical source files are under v03/results/main. summary.csv reports denominators and counts by split, condition and method; strata.csv adds topology, signal and quorum; effects.csv has paired intervals; ceiling.csv is an exploratory feasibility envelope. language.csv contains each estimate and abstention. semantically contradictory predicted masks trigger abstention. Eligibility and postdelivery fault interventions are deterministic functions of each job; primary statistical resampling uses seed blocks. Clock-time comparisons use medians on the release host and are descriptive. Seven runs of the same method/job across conditions do not constitute seven independent jobs.
