# Frozen final-study protocol
Freeze before implementing decision rules or scoring. Unit is a fictional dossier requiring an early quorum of signed reviews, a later certificate from every assigned reviewer, and occasionally a commissioned staff memo. Distinct roles may share one mailbox. This is a set-cover and information-intersection task: early evidence can be spread among interchangeable reviewers and a recipient can infer one of four sites across different language forms. Known eligible reviewers constrain the late assignment; exact assignments and subsequent withdrawal are hidden. Compare greedy, a minimum-quorum baseline, a legacy cue adapter and a planner that first allocates votes outside the future-eligible set. Protect accumulated knowledge with typed evidence. Compare checked, unchecked and language-filtered versions; ablate history. No tuning on validation or test.
 
H1: minimum-quorum planning with current eligibility improves safe exact completion over greedy on clean test jobs. H2: it improves over fixed-order minimum quorum on the same jobs. H3: verified evidence reduces any forbidden delivery versus the same unchecked planner under combined faulty local metadata. H4: successful but wrong attestation increases exposure versus the same verified mixed regime with honest attestation. Evaluate paired risk differences across 12 seed blocks; percentile seed bootstrap 10,000 draws, seed 303, 98.75% two-sided Bonferroni intervals; criterion is strict exclusion of zero in the specified direction. Explore cues, mailbox aliasing and single faults descriptively. Every block has 24 distinct jobs, each replayed across identical interventions. Stop at frozen blocks, including null and negative outcomes.

Text study: distinguish literal site strings, assertions of one attribute, negation, summaries, harmless mentions, composition, and unresolved scope/attribution. A template author supplies intended denotations as sets of possible sites. Hold out whole wording templates. Compare a literal detector, compositional dictionary and development-only statistical classifier. Evaluate exposure at a named public observer given prior knowledge. Report confusion counts, abstentions and accuracy on determinate gold; explicitly ambiguous items have no automatic gold exposure label. No human adjudication or general semantic accuracy claim. No model tokens in scripted study. If local generative weights/runtime unavailable, deliver a local pilot with fixed paired prompts/actions; no remote paid service.
