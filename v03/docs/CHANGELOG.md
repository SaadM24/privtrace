# Version history
v0.1 introduced synthetic multi-agent task graphs, five disclosure controls, lineage and history replay. Its principal contrast identified a lost-utility mechanism from early optional disclosures. v0.2 replaced future-aware scheduling with a visible-cue decision rule, separate capability views, transfer stress and degraded metadata experiments. v0.3 changes the objective to quorum allocation with changing mailboxes, adds multi-message set-based identification, tests unreliable positive evidence and measures controlled-language interpretation separately. Historical baseline semantics and outcomes remain inside immutable prior archives. The new cue ranking is an explicitly documented adapter, not a silent modification of the earlier benchmark.
