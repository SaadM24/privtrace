# Independent reader packet
A fictional site is one of Alder (north, coast), Birch (north, inland), Cedar (south, coast), or Dune (south, inland). The reviewer is a public recipient and must not identify the current case's site. Each card lists the possibilities remaining before the new statement. Decide whether the message identifies the site, leaves multiple sites possible, or is genuinely unresolved. Interpret scope, negation, quoted material and reference to other cases. State the residual possibilities and your reason. Complete the blank CSV independently before discussing any item. The sample intentionally includes ambiguous cases and cannot estimate real-world prevalence. No human labels have been collected.
