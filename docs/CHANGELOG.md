# Changes since v0.1
| Area | v0.1 | v0.2 |
|---|---|---|
| Decision timing | Exploratory scheduler knew whether a later receipt was required | Immutable current view, early expiring opportunity and hidden later demand; optional closed-ticket estimator across jobs |
| Experiments | Primary scripted graphs plus one fresh-seed intervention | Paired clean/degraded metadata, isolated calibration, validation, test, museum transfer and fresh distribution-change streams |
| Authorization | Synchronous grant and trusted lineage | Archived adjudicator reused byte-for-byte; gateways may see stale grants and incorrect labels. Checked variants can request current verification when available |
| Evaluation | Planted leakage, completion, replay | Adds safe exact completion, offline optimum independently checked in closed form, avoidable single-denial tests, seed-block intervals and allocation probes |
| Delivery | Research archive with blinded-review coordinator material | That archive remains intact in the owner's private package. New standalone PDFs contain no reviewer secrets |

No historical scores were patched. Version numbers refer to separate workloads; differences in raw completion rates between releases are not a longitudinal improvement estimate.
