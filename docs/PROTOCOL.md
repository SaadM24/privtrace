# Locked before implementing planners or observing v0.2 outcomes
Unit: a synthetic job. The initial optional A expires before the final job ticket is opened. A target needs A, B, both, or neither. Late B is offered only when requested. Distinct A and B at the same public observer reveal a protected binary attribute. Both at a single public observer can make exact success incompatible with privacy. A prespecified auxiliary document may also be required by a staff observer. Two metadata regimes replay identical exogenous jobs. Clean has accurate current information. Degraded has delayed notifications, wrong or absent ancestry claims and sometimes unavailable live verification. Candidate arrivals are asynchronous.logical ticks; mediation at the receiving gateway is atomic.

Fit one table on development tickets only, estimating the probability of A-only vs B-only demand given the public dispatch cue, with additive smoothing. Freeze its contents before validation/test scoring. No validation-based tuning. Greedy local/full reuse the archived adjudicator; conservative skips optional target A; utility plans with conditional expected exact completion. Checked utility additionally verifies current grant and resource ancestry for auxiliary documents, failing closed on unavailable proof. Ablate the cue and live verification, and run greedy with live verification alone. The offline oracle knows all arrivals, grants and roots and searches safe subsets for maximum exact completion. No online callable may receive scenario, seed, identifier, future ticket, truth, future event objects or gold labels. Record immutable decision-time fields. Test identical prefixes against divergent futures. Separate current-time live service responses from future oracle access.

Primary evaluation uses held-out in-family jobs with clean metadata. H1: utility improves exact completion relative to greedy full; H2: utility improves completion relative to conservative. Accept each if lower bound of paired interval exceeds zero. H3: under degraded metadata, checked utility reduces any exposure vs utility; require upper bound below zero. Use paired seed-block bootstrap, 10,000 replicates, fixed seed 209, 98.333% two-sided percentile intervals (Bonferroni family of three). Report absolute risk differences. Describe excess blocking and reversals even when privacy improves. A single reconstructed share does not count as inference. Report clean and degraded results on development, validation, test, and a separately specified transfer family. No external authorship claimed. Report oracle headroom on safe success; unsafe completion may exceed the ceiling. No optimizer may select settings on transfer results. Record latency, separate traced Python allocation, and omitted LLM trials.

Transfer family: a two-stage museum loan uses an early packing token and later courier ticket, with a custody handoff that may replace the booking and invert the usual association of visible urgency with the required document. Generate its cases in separate code using a finite manifest of booking changes, independent of the development sampler. Freeze both specifications before calibration. The release tests generalization to this authored shift only.
